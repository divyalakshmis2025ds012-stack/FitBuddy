# Problem Statement

Many people who want to start exercising do not have a structured plan that matches their goal and preferred intensity. Hiring a trainer is costly, and generic plans found online are not personalised or easy to adjust.

**FitBuddy** solves this by using Google Gemini to generate a personalised 7-day workout plan (warm-up, main workout, cooldown for each day) plus a short nutrition tip. The user can send feedback and get the plan revised, and an admin can review all users and their plans.

| Who | What they need |
|---|---|
| Beginner / regular exerciser | A day-by-day plan that fits their goal and intensity (low / medium / high) |
| User who finds the plan too hard or not suitable | A way to give feedback and get an updated plan |
| Admin | A single page listing all users and their plans |
